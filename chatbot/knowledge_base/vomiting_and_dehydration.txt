SYMPTOMS: vomiting, nausea, inability to keep fluids down, signs of dehydration

OVERVIEW
Vomiting that prevents oral intake risks dehydration and electrolyte loss, especially in children.

WHAT TO DO
- Start oral rehydration solutions; seek urgent care if unable to keep fluids or if severe.
- For infants and elderly, seek prompt medical review.

WARNING SIGNS
- Lethargy, sunken eyes, very reduced urine, rapid breathing, persistent vomiting.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Small sips of ORS frequently; avoid fatty or spicy foods until recovery.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not give large volumes at once; avoid antiemetics without advice for children.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Access to ORS, when to visit nearest clinic or hospital.

REFERENCES
- WHO rehydration guidance

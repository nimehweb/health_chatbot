SYMPTOMS: generalized weakness, fatigue, malaise, reduced activity

OVERVIEW
Weakness and fatigue can be caused by infections, chronic disease, dehydration, or other causes.

WHAT TO DO
- Rest, hydrate, seek care if sudden severe weakness, difficulty walking, or associated chest pain.
- Consider outpatient review for persistent fatigue lasting weeks.

WARNING SIGNS
- Rapidly progressive weakness, inability to move limbs, breathing difficulty, fainting.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Gradual return to activity, maintain nutrition and hydration, avoid strenuous exertion.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not ignore sudden inability to move or perform daily tasks.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Local clinics, when to seek hospital assessment.

REFERENCES
- Primary care guidance

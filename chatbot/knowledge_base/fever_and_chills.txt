SYMPTOMS: fever, chills, sweating, body aches, elevated temperature

OVERVIEW
Brief description of fever and chills as a symptom complex and common causes in Nigeria.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek care if fever is high (>39°C) or persistent beyond 48 hours.
- Get tested where available (malaria RDT, COVID/other tests as appropriate).

WARNING SIGNS
- Confusion, inability to drink, seizures, difficulty breathing, persistent vomiting.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Stay hydrated, rest, use paracetamol as advised, avoid aspirin in children.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not give antibiotics without prescription; avoid unverified traditional remedies.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Where to seek care (primary health centre, pharmacy clinics, NCDC resources).

REFERENCES

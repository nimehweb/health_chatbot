SYMPTOMS: red eye, pain, discharge, changes in vision, blurry vision

OVERVIEW
Eye symptoms range from conjunctivitis to sight-threatening emergencies; vision changes need urgent assessment.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek urgent eye care for sudden vision loss, severe pain, or chemical exposure.
- For mild conjunctivitis, maintain hygiene and seek primary care.

WARNING SIGNS
- Sudden loss of vision, severe eye pain, pupil irregularity.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Avoid rubbing, use clean compresses, avoid contact lens use until assessed.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not self-instill unverified eye drops; avoid traditional eye medicines.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Eye clinics and referral pathways in tertiary hospitals.

REFERENCES
- WHO eye health resources

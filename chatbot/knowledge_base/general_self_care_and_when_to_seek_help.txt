SYMPTOMS: general advice covering multiple mild symptoms

OVERVIEW
Guidance for common mild conditions and when escalation is needed.

WHAT TO DO
- Rest, hydrate, use over-the-counter analgesics appropriately, monitor symptoms.
- Seek care if symptoms worsen, persist beyond expected time, or new red flags appear.

WARNING SIGNS
- Any red flag listed in other articles (fever >39°C, severe pain, breathing difficulty).

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Simple home care measures, when to use paracetamol, when to use ORS.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Avoid unnecessary antibiotics and unverified traditional treatments.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Use primary health centres for initial care; when to go to hospital.

REFERENCES
- WHO, NCDC

SYMPTOMS: heavy bleeding, deep wounds, loss of consciousness, fractures

OVERVIEW
Major injuries and uncontrolled bleeding require immediate emergency care and first aid.

WHAT TO DO
- Apply direct pressure to bleeding, call emergency services, and seek trauma-capable facility.

WARNING SIGNS
- Uncontrolled bleeding, altered consciousness, severe deformity, pale clammy skin.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Apply pressure, immobilize limbs, keep person warm.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not remove deeply embedded objects; do not use tourniquets unless trained.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Emergency numbers and trauma centres.

REFERENCES
- Basic trauma life support guidance

SYMPTOMS: rash, itching, redness, discharge, blisters

OVERVIEW
Skin rashes have many causes from infections to allergic reactions; identify red flags for severe infections.

WHAT TO DO
- If spreading rapidly, painful, or with fever, seek medical review; for mild rashes try barrier care and antihistamines.

WARNING SIGNS
- Rapid spread with fever, pus discharge, necrosis, severe pain.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Keep area clean, avoid scratching, use emollients for dryness.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Avoid popping blisters or applying unverified topical remedies.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Common tropical skin infections and when to see dermatology or primary care.

REFERENCES
- WHO dermatology guidance

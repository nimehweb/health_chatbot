SYMPTOMS: headache, migraine, dizziness, weakness, numbness, confusion

OVERVIEW
Headache patterns vary from tension and migraine to signs of neurologic emergencies.

WHAT TO DO
- If a new severe (“worst ever”) headache, sudden onset, or neurological signs, seek emergency care.
- For recurrent headaches, arrange outpatient review.

WARNING SIGNS
- Sudden severe headache, weakness on one side, slurred speech, loss of consciousness.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Rest in a quiet dark room, hydration, simple analgesia if appropriate.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Avoid mixing multiple pain medications; avoid driving if dizzy/confused.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- When to access emergency services and neurology follow-up options.

REFERENCES
- WHO neurological emergency guidance

SYMPTOMS: joint pain, swelling, stiffness, reduced range of motion

OVERVIEW
Joint pain may be due to injury, inflammatory conditions, infection, or chronic disease.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek medical review for severe pain, fever, or inability to use the joint; for mild pain, rest and analgesia.

WARNING SIGNS
- Fever with joint swelling, rapidly worsening pain, signs of sepsis.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Rest the joint, apply ice, use elevation and analgesia as needed.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not apply heat to acute swelling without advice; avoid heavy loading.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- When to access orthopaedic or rheumatology services.

REFERENCES
- Primary care guidance

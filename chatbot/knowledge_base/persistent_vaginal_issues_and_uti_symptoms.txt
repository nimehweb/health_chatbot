SYMPTOMS: painful urination, increased frequency, lower abdominal pain, abnormal discharge

OVERVIEW
Urinary tract infections and vaginal infections are common and often treatable; seek care for accurate diagnosis.

WHAT TO DO
- See a clinician for testing and targeted treatment; maintain hydration.
- Avoid unprotected sex until treated if infection suspected.

WARNING SIGNS
- Fever with flank pain, vomiting, blood in urine, signs of systemic illness.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Good hygiene, avoid irritants, maintain fluid intake.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not self-prescribe antibiotics without testing.

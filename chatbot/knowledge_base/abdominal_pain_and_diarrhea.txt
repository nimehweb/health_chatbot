SYMPTOMS: abdominal pain, cramping, diarrhea, bloating, fever

OVERVIEW
Abdominal pain with diarrhea can be due to infections, food poisoning, or chronic conditions.

WHAT TO DO
- Stay hydrated, use oral rehydration solutions for diarrhea, seek care for severe or persistent symptoms.
- If high fever or blood in stool, see a clinician.

WARNING SIGNS
- Severe dehydration, blood in stool, persistent high fever, severe abdominal tenderness.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Sip ORS, avoid anti-diarrheal meds in presence of blood or high fever unless advised.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Avoid giving children adult medications without medical advice.

- Community health options, safe water practices, when to visit hospital.

REFERENCES

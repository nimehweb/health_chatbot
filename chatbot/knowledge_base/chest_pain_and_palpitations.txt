SYMPTOMS: chest pain, chest tightness, palpitations, shortness of breath

OVERVIEW
Chest pain can be cardiac, respiratory, gastrointestinal or musculoskeletal; treat as potentially serious.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek immediate emergency care if chest pain is severe, sudden, or accompanied by breathlessness.
- Call emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.

WARNING SIGNS
- Collapse, sweating, radiating arm/jaw pain, fainting, severe breathlessness.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Stop activity, sit or lie comfortably, do not drive yourself if possible.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not ignore severe chest pain; do not self-prescribe heart medications without evaluation.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Emergency numbers, nearest tertiary hospitals with cardiology services.

REFERENCES
- WHO, local cardiac care guidelines

SYMPTOMS: thoughts of self-harm, severe depression, psychosis, sudden behavioural change

OVERVIEW
Mental health crises can be dangerous and require prompt support and sometimes emergency care.

WHAT TO DO
- If risk to self or others, seek emergency care or call crisis lines immediately.
- For non-immediate crises, contact mental health services or trusted support.

WARNING SIGNS
- Active suicidal thoughts, severe agitation, inability to care for self.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Stay with the person, remove means of harm, contact emergency services.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not leave someone at immediate risk alone; do not minimise their feelings.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Local mental health resources, crisis numbers, community support organisations.

REFERENCES
- WHO mental health resources

SYMPTOMS: vaginal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, decreased fetal movement, severe headache

OVERVIEW
Pregnancy-related red flags require urgent obstetric assessment to protect mother and baby.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek immediate care at an antenatal clinic or emergency obstetric unit for any bleeding or severe pain.

WARNING SIGNS
- Heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, fainting, reduced fetal movements.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Lie on the left side if possible, avoid heavy activity, arrange transport.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not use traditional remedies that delay care; avoid heavy lifting.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Where to access maternity units and emergency obstetric care.

REFERENCES
- WHO maternal health guidance

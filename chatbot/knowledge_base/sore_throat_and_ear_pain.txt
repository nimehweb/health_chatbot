SYMPTOMS: sore throat, ear pain, difficulty swallowing, muffled hearing

OVERVIEW
Common ENT problems include viral pharyngitis, otitis media, and tonsillitis; differentiate red flags.

WHAT TO DO
- If mild, hydrate, rest, and use analgesia. Seek care for severe throat pain, drooling, or breathing difficulty.

WARNING SIGNS
- Stridor, drooling, severe swelling, muffled voice, high fever.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Warm fluids for adults, analgesics, avoid irritants like smoke.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Avoid home remedies that block the airway or delay care for severe symptoms.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Local clinics and ENT referral pathways.

REFERENCES
- WHO ENT guidance

SYMPTOMS: cough, shortness of breath, wheeze, chest tightness

OVERVIEW
Cough and breathlessness may reflect infections, asthma, pneumonia or cardiac causes.

WHAT TO DO
- If mild, monitor; if moderate or worse, seek medical assessment and oxygen evaluation.
- Avoid exertion and get medical review for rapid worsening.

WARNING SIGNS
- Severe shortness of breath, inability to speak full sentences, cyanosis, fainting.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Sit upright, rest, use inhaler if previously prescribed and available.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not ignore progressive breathlessness; avoid sedatives that depress breathing.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Where to seek emergency oxygen-capable facilities; local referral guidance.

REFERENCES
- WHO, local health ministry guidance

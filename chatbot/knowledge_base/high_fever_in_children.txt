SYMPTOMS: high fever, irritability, poor feeding, lethargy, convulsions

OVERVIEW
High fever in children can be serious; infants and young children dehydrate quickly and may have febrile seizures.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek urgent medical care for infants under 3 months with any fever, or if the child is very lethargic.
- Use age-appropriate paracetamol and ensure hydration while arranging care.

WARNING SIGNS
- Poor feeding, limpness, persistent vomiting, convulsions, difficulty breathing.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Keep child lightly clothed, give small regular fluids, avoid aspirin.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not delay seeking care for babies; do not give adult medications to children.

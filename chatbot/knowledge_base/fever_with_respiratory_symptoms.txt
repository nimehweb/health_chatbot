SYMPTOMS: fever, cough, sore throat, nasal congestion, runny nose

OVERVIEW
Fever with respiratory symptoms can indicate viral or bacterial infections; evaluate for breathing difficulty.

WHAT TO DO
- Monitor breathing and temperature; seek care if shortness of breath or worsening symptoms.
- Request appropriate tests where available (e.g., RDTs, chest assessment).

WARNING SIGNS
- Rapid breathing, persistent chest pain, blue lips/face, severe wheeze.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Rest, hydrate, use paracetamol for fever, isolate if contagious respiratory illness suspected.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Avoid giving cough syrups to very young children without medical advice.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- Local clinic referral, when to visit emergency department, community health workers.

REFERENCES
- NCDC, WHO

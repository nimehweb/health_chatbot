SYMPTOMS: painful urination, blood in urine, lower abdominal pain, frequent urination

OVERVIEW
Hematuria and painful urination can indicate infection, stones or other causes that need evaluation.

WHAT TO DO
- Seek medical assessment for testing (urinalysis, urine culture) and targeted treatment.

WARNING SIGNS
- High fever, flank pain, vomiting, severe dehydration.

SELF CARE WHILE SEEKING HELP
- Hydrate, avoid irritant fluids like alcohol, seek prompt care for blood in urine.

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not ignore visible blood in urine; avoid self-medicating with antibiotics.

NIGERIAN CONTEXT
- When to access urology or emergency services.
